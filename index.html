A Few Things I've Built :)

Hi, I'm Paweł Zięba. This is where I collect projects that showcase my technical skills in a more accessible way than raw code on GitHub ;)
It's a short overview of what I do, what I'm currently learning, and some of the projects I've built in my spare time.


A little about me:


I'm an electronics engineer. In my day-to-day work, I design electronic systems from schematic design and multilayer PCBs, through hardware bring-up and testing, all the way to preparing the final design for production.

I earned my Bachelor's degree in Microelectronics in Technology and Medicine at AGH University of Krakow, and I'm currently working on my Master's thesis.

I'm developing my skills in embedded systems, both on microcontrollers and in Embedded Linux environments. I'm particularly interested in Linux, the Yocto Project, and building systems tailored to specific hardware platforms.

I enjoy working at the intersection of hardware and system-level software, especially in the context of SoC platforms.
